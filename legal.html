Privacy Policy

In short: Widgetdown doesn't collect any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your countdowns are stored on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud. We can't see them.

Who we are

Widgetdown is an iPhone app made by Jake Bailey, an independent developer in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website.

What the app collects

Nothing. Widgetdown has no servers of its own and doesn't send your information to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your device

The countdowns you create (their titles, emoji, dates, styles, milestones and reminder settings) are saved on your device, where the app and its widgets can read them. If you add a photo to a countdown, the app keeps a resized copy of it for the widget. Deleting a countdown or the app removes this information from your device.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Widgetdown backs your countdowns and their photos up to your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app and stay in step on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to your iCloud account.

You can turn iCloud sync off on a device in the app's Settings, or keep an individual countdown on one device only. To delete Widgetdown's iCloud data, use the iCloud storage settings on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Photos

When you choose a background photo, you pick it with Apple's photo picker. Widgetdown only receives the photo you choose and can't see the rest of your photo library.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Asking for permission and delivering them is handled by iOS; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing

When you share a countdown as a picture, or send it as a link for a friend to add, you choose who to send it to. A shared link contains that countdown's title, emoji, dates, design and milestones (not its photo, reminders or other countdowns), so anyone who receives the link can read them.

Information Apple provides

Apple handles purchases on the App Store and gives us sales reports that don't identify you. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may also share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us, which we use only to fix problems and improve the app. You can change this in Settings under Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. The company hosting it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and we don't share them.

Children

Widgetdown doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal data, such as seeing or deleting it. Because Widgetdown doesn't collect your data, everything is in your own hands: you can view, change or delete your countdowns in the app, and manage your iCloud data in your device's settings. If you have a question, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Widgetdown handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
