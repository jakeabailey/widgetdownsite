Privacy Policy

In short: Widgetdown doesn't collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your countdowns stay on your devices and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account, which we can't access.

Who we are

Widgetdown is an app for iPhone (and, when released, Mac) made by Jake Bailey, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us", "our"). This policy covers the Widgetdown app, its widgets and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. Widgetdown has no servers and never sends information to us. The only place it sends anything is your own iCloud account, if you use it (see iCloud). It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs): it's built only with Apple's own frameworks. We don't sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act or any other law. On the App Store, Widgetdown's privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Widgetdown is stored on your device, where the app and its widgets can read it: your countdowns' titles, emoji, dates, designs, colours and fonts, milestones, repeat and reminder choices, and the app's settings. If you add a photo to a countdown, the app keeps a resized copy of it. Deleting a countdown removes it, and deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud and leave Sync with iCloud turned on (it's on by default), Widgetdown backs your countdowns and their photos up to your own iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app and stay the same across your devices. This data travels to and is stored by Apple, under Apple's iCloud Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. It's in your account, not ours: we can't see, access or recover it.

You control it:

  • Turn sync off on one device: in Widgetdown, open Settings and turn off Sync with iCloud. Your iCloud copy is left as it is.
  • Keep one countdown off iCloud: open the countdown and turn off Sync with iCloud. It's removed from iCloud and kept on that device only.
  • Stop Widgetdown using iCloud altogether: on iPhone, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and turn off iCloud Drive for Widgetdown.
  • Delete Widgetdown's iCloud data: on iPhone, go to Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (or Storage), choose Widgetdown and delete its data. On a Mac, use System Settings, then your Apple Account, then iCloud. The exact wording can vary between versions of iOS and macOS.

Photos

Background photos are chosen with Apple's photo picker. Widgetdown only receives the photos you pick, never the rest of your library, and doesn't need permission to read your photo library.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications that iOS schedules and delivers on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off for each countdown, for each device in Widgetdown's Settings, or in your device's notification settings.

Sharing

You can share a countdown as a picture, or send it as a link for a friend to add. You choose the app and the people you share with, and that app's own privacy policy applies. A shared link contains only that countdown's title, emoji, dates, repeat setting, design and the names of its milestones (not its photo, reminders, which milestones are ticked off, or any other countdown), and anyone who receives it can read them. Nothing passes through us.

Information Apple provides to developers

Apple runs the App Store and processes your purchase. Apple gives us sales figures and, only if you've agreed to share them with developers, crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. We use these only to fix problems and improve the app. You can change what you share in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Analytics & Improvements. When Widgetdown asks if you'd like to rate it, the request is handled by Apple and nothing is sent to us.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

If you email us

If you contact us, we'll have your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, don't share it, and delete it when it's no longer needed or when you ask. This is the only personal data we may ever hold about you.

Children

Widgetdown doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children of any age. Because it collects nothing, children's countdowns stay on their own devices and in their own (or their family's) iCloud, like everyone else's.

Your rights

Data protection laws, including the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act, give you rights such as access to, correction of and deletion of your personal data. Because we don't hold any data from the app, there's nothing for us to give you or delete: everything is already in your hands. To remove all of Widgetdown's data, delete the app from each of your devices and delete its iCloud data as described in iCloud above. If you've emailed us, you can ask us to delete your messages. If you're unhappy with how we've handled a privacy matter, please contact us first; you can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the dates at the top. If Widgetdown ever changes how it handles data in a way that matters, such as collecting something it doesn't today, we'll say so clearly here and in the app before the change applies.
